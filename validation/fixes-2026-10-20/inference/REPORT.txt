Inference repair and fresh review, 2026-10-20

Scope
F01 (empty-unit robust safeguards and CR1), F04 (direct-effect predictor
origins), and adjacent verified defects involving zero sampling weights,
target-specific missingness, and legacy empty-unit counting.

Implementation
1. R/inference-service.R supplies the independent-group selection. Zero
   weights never count. Guard/rank checks always require genuinely observed
   measurement; CR1 alone respects the explicit drop_empty=FALSE convention.
   Score matrices preserve all rows for group alignment.
2. Profile, membership-covariate, homogeneous-transition and general-LTA
   engines use contributing groups for robust validity and CR1. Rank checks
   exclude empty/zero-weight rows. Additive, regression and growth engines
   count positive-weight independent units for their safeguards and CR1.
3. Direct effects use centred predictor coordinates, matching their fitting
   chart. The full covariance, information and scores are transformed back
   to the reported predictor origin, including intercept/slope cross terms.
   Stationarity diagnostics remain in conditioned coordinates. Conditioned
   information/scores are exposed for VLMR without dropping aligned rows.
4. Additional review found that an irrelevant covariate on a missing target
   could destabilize fitting itself. A direct effect now gets centres and
   widths from the positive-weight observations of its own target. Internal
   designs can therefore repeat a predictor column with different centres;
   the public fit retains the original unique covariate columns and metadata.

Reproduced additional failures before repair
- Adding ten entirely missing groups with predictors at +/-1e5 changed a
  regular one-profile likelihood from about -549.8 to -649.6 and prevented
  convergence. Sharing a predictor across targets with different observation
  patterns also prevented convergence. Both regressions pass after repair.
- A single informative group, fixed means, one free variance and twenty
  empty groups under drop_empty=FALSE produced a robust variance SE of
  1.90e-16 despite the rank check (a tiny scalar has numerical rank one).
  The observed-unit validity guard now refuses this case.

Independent references
- One-profile direct-effect intercepts/slopes and the complete covariance
  agree with lm() normal maximum-likelihood references after shifts of
  +2026 and -1000. The maximum relative covariance discrepancy in the
  public proof is 3.54e-14. The shifted intercept SE is 120.87500645;
  slope SEs remain 0.05295047 and 0.05219989.
- A separately constructed Gaussian group-score sandwich agrees with the
  reported robust covariance, including the shifted intercept cross terms.
- Target-specific missingness is compared separately against each outcome's
  independent lm() fit and complete intercept/slope covariance.
- One informative group plus 95 empty groups refuses robust and OPG
  inference. With five informative groups, adding 95 empty groups leaves
  the mean SE at 0.03272652.

Review boundaries
Direct effects publicly refuse fixed/start, membership covariates, time,
non-diagonal covariance and indicator centring/scaling. Those refusals are
preserved; the fix does not imply support for those combinations. Existing
ordinary fixed-measurement tests were rerun separately. No new loops,
dependencies, commits, pushes or project tracking-log edits were introduced
by this subtask.
