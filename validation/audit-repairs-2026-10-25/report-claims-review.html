Guarantees that need narrower wording

Numerical and API correctness: finite tests and source review cannot establish “free of conceptual flaws,” universal unit invariance, or bit-for-bit preservation of every return structure and default. The EM convergence flag combines a likelihood tolerance with an optional inner-solver flag; that is not a general proof of a stationary point or global maximum. R/kernel-em.R:177.

The count-only CR1 effect is bounded. Section 6.3 attributes standard errors near 1e−17 to counting empty groups. Under its own displayed multiplier, holding information A, score cross-product B and parameter units fixed, the corrected/old SE ratio is √[(G*/(G*−1))/(G/(G−1))]. For G ≥ G* ≥ 2, this lies between 1 and √2, strictly below √2 for finite counts. At 60 informative and 100 counted groups, the ratio is about 1.003384. Thus this multiplier alone cannot explain orders-of-magnitude deflation; information, scores, units or other effects need separate evidence. This conditional calculation does not attribute every actual empty-group defect to that multiplier or reinterpret the separate nobs/R3STEP repairs. Derivation and closed-form checks.

Conditions and random state: the assertion that no generic errors exist is contradicted by ordinary stopifnot() validations. A NULL seed intentionally advances the caller's stream; an explicit seed scopes the exposed state, with a documented Box-Muller cache limitation.
