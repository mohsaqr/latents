What remains limited

  • Optimization: finite searches can miss a higher maximum. The original direct-effects and joint-item default-fit gaps remain visible; the verification recipes do not certify every default fit.
  • RNG and precision: R’s hidden Box–Muller cache cannot be restored through .Random.seed, and extremely large origins retain finite-double limits.
  • Verification scope: commercial comparisons use retained Mplus/Latent GOLD outputs. Historical studies were not all regenerated; live Windows/Linux behavior is not certified.
  • Guide execution: eval=FALSE chunks remained unevaluated. The earlier 99-bootstrap growth example is pre-repair evidence, not a final-source rerun.
  • Claude: its CLI was logged out. Independent agent and numerical reviews were used instead.
