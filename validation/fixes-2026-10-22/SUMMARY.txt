Fixes for the five confirmed issues reviewed at 12e0145 (latents 0.96.2)
2026-10-22

Implemented

1. Staged bootstrap refits measurement using that stage's own search controls
   before refitting stage two. A one-start second stage no longer discards
   the first stage's explicit emEM pool. Start-count overrides resolve the
   appropriate pool independently for each stage. Supplied single-level
   measurement and differently named identifier/time columns are supported.
   Source: R/bootstrap-inference.R.

2. Thin-profile parameter counts exclude covariances held at zero by a local
   dependence pattern. Five Gaussian indicators and one covariance count as
   eleven density parameters per profile, rather than twenty.
   Source: R/multilpa.R.

3. Held means are excluded from the count, and held variances disable the
   collapse diagnostic. Fixed measurement is no longer falsely flagged.
   Source: R/multilpa.R.

4. Both LTA engines count only rows with an observed Gaussian indicator for
   the Gaussian warning. All-empty and categorical-only rows cannot hide a
   thin Gaussian profile, independently of the drop_empty reporting policy.
   Weighted fits use the saved weighted E-step memberships rather than raw
   individual posterior probabilities.
   Sources: R/transitions.R, R/lta-engine.R.

5. LTA occasion-specific measurement is checked per separately estimated
   measurement block. Warnings identify the occasion position, consistent
   with the occasion numbering of the measurement results table.
   Source: R/lta-engine.R.
