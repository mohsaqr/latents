Holding all other fitted parameters fixed and moving that single rare-move
logit toward the boundary improves the ORIGINAL likelihood:

  logit -11.955139: p = 6.426082e-6; reference LL -5293.183467038531.
  logit -13.955139: p = 8.696805e-7; LL improves by 3.811266e-5.
  logit -27.955139: p = 7.231649e-13; LL improves by 4.407785e-5.

This is a conditional likelihood-profile statement, not a proof that every
start has the same global mixture maximum. It establishes that the original
test's current solution has a direction of improvement crossing the guard:
slightly different optimizer arithmetic/stopping can legitimately classify
its rare move as a boundary. An earlier pass does not establish interiority.

Principled replacement

The final deterministic fixture enumerates every one of the 16 binary state
paths at four occasions, with ten copies per path and identical symmetric
normal-quantile residuals per path. It uses state means +/-0.1 and residual
scale 0.05. This is a designed interior transition problem, not a changed
seed selected to evade the guard. Assertions explicitly require convergence,
no boundary flags and initial/transition probabilities within (0.25,0.75).

Independent runs under all three base-R matrix-product modes pass:

  default/internal/blas LL = 620.1129242380422;
  initial probability = 0.5000000000000;
  transition probabilities = 0.4960078 to 0.4960179;
  no fit/probability boundary, converged;
  max SE change between steps 0.01 and 0.0001 = 5.64747e-7;
  reported transition SE versus returned covariance discrepancy = EXACT zero.

The original step sizes, 1e-12 covariance-consistency tolerance and inequality
between the coarse/fine tables remain intact. A new >1e-8 sensitivity assertion
gives a material margin beyond rounding. No production boundary rule, fitting
method or inference tolerance is changed.
