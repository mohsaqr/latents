# Self-regulated learning scores simulated by a large language model

Scores on five constructs of the Motivated Strategies for Learning
Questionnaire (MSLQ) for 300 simulated respondents. Each score is the
mean of the construct's Likert items, on a scale from 1 to 7. The
responses were **generated by a large language model (GPT)**, not
collected from students: they come from a study of how language models
answer psychological surveys, and are used here because they are a
well-structured, openly shareable cross-sectional example. They should
not be read as evidence about students.

## Usage

``` r
srl
```

## Format

A data frame with 300 rows and 5 columns:

- `cognitive_strategies`:

  cognitive strategy use

- `intrinsic_value`:

  intrinsic value of the course

- `self_efficacy`:

  self-efficacy

- `self_regulation`:

  self-regulation

- `test_anxiety`:

  test anxiety

## Source

The `SRL_GPT` data of the psychnets package, with descriptive column
names. Saqr, M. (2025). Delving into the psychology of machines:
Exploring the structure of self-regulated learning via LLM-generated
survey responses. *Computers in Human Behavior*, 173, 108769.
[doi:10.1016/j.chb.2025.108769](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2025.108769)

## Details

The five constructs are strongly correlated (about 0.8, except test
anxiety), which makes the data a good illustration of how the choice of
covariance structure shapes a latent profile analysis.
