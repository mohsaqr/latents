disen.csv: transcribed 2026-11-14 from Appendix A of Andruff et al. (2009),
Tutorials in Quantitative Methods for Psychology 5(1), 11-24 (the PDF the owner
supplied, p011.pdf), by pdftotext -layout and a parser that requires the ids
1 to 107 in order with three two-decimal scores each. Rows 97 to 107 agree
with the SAS data window of the paper's Figure 1, and two printed log
likelihoods (-300.45, -255.59) are reproduced from these values.
The printed PROC TRAJ values in compare-functions.R are read from Figures 3
and 4 (screenshots of SAS output) and from the text, pp. 17-18.
