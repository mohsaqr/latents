## Fixtures not used, and why

- The class-enumeration Monte Carlo (`lta-mc-paper-results.json` with
  `lta-mc-lpa-*.json`): the reference is Mplus's selection rates on the
  paper's own datasets. The available datasets are new R draws, and the Mplus
  variance specification is not recorded, so agreement could only be
  distributional and would not tell a wrong specification apart from a wrong
  implementation. A 30-dataset pilot of the K = 3, 4, 5 grid did not finish
  in 35 minutes, and the full 300 datasets are out of reach for a test. JStats
  attributes this material to Mews, Koslik & Langrock (2024); that title is
  about how latent Markov models handle time and space, so check the citation
  before using it.

- The `lta-mc-std-*` cells simulate a different transition matrix at each of
  the two steps (`tau_t1_t2` differs from `tau_t2_t3`). `fit_transitions()`
  assumes one matrix for all occasions, so it would be fitting the wrong model
  and there is nothing to be equivalent to.
- The random-intercept (RI-LTA) row, the time-varying, multigroup, Bayesian,
  Poisson, ordinal, mover-stayer and covariate LTA fixtures describe models
  that multilpa does not fit.
- Entropy: Mplus's LTA entropy is computed over each person's joint latent
  path. multilpa computes it per observation, so the two numbers measure
  different things and are not compared.
